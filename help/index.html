How to stop autopay on Study With Cat

You can stop future monthly renewals from your Study With Cat account.

  1. Open your study room and choose My desk.
  2. Choose My account. If you’re signed out, sign in with the same Google account you used when subscribing.
  3. Select Stop monthly renewal and confirm.
  4. Wait for the confirmation that monthly renewal is stopped. Your account shows the date your paid access ends.
Will I lose premium immediately if I cancel?

No. Stopping renewal keeps your paid features available through the paid-access date shown in My account. After that, you can continue using the free Study Buddy desk. Cancel before your next scheduled payment and check that the cancellation confirmation appears.

Can I cancel a UPI AutoPay mandate from my UPI app?

If you authorised the subscription with UPI, you can also manage its mandate in the UPI app you used. Look for its AutoPay, Mandates, or Subscriptions section, select the matching mandate, and follow that app’s cancellation steps. Labels vary by app. Check that the cancellation succeeded. Razorpay explains UPI subscription cancellation.

For a cancellation problem or an unclear account status, contact our support email with your account email and subscription ID.

Does signing out or closing the website stop autopay?

No. Signing out, closing a tab, or clearing browser data does not cancel a subscription. Use Stop monthly renewal in My account, or cancel the UPI mandate in your UPI app if you paid through UPI.

What if the stop-renewal button is missing or shows an error?

Check that you are signed in with the Google account used for the purchase. If your account already says monthly renewal is stopped, there is nothing more to confirm on the website. If you cannot find your subscription or the cancellation fails, email meowmeow1234788@gmail.com so we can investigate. An error message is not confirmation that renewal has stopped.

Monthly payments, activation & billing help

How much does Study With Cat premium cost?

Study Kitten is ₹59 per month and Scholar Cat is ₹99 per month, including the first month. These are recurring monthly subscriptions. Razorpay displays the amount and renewal terms before you authorise payment. There is no current launch discount or free paid-plan trial.

Who processes my payment? Can I pay with UPI or a card?

Payments are processed through Razorpay checkout. Use one of the methods it offers for your subscription; available methods can depend on your bank, app, and eligibility. UPI AutoPay and recurring card payments require you to authorise a recurring payment arrangement. Study With Cat does not receive or store your full card details, UPI PIN, or payment OTP.

I paid, but premium hasn’t unlocked. What should I do?

Sign in with the Google account used for the purchase, then open My desk → My account to refresh your membership. Premium activates after the server confirms a successful subscription payment; a pending or failed payment does not unlock it. If your bank shows a successful debit and your account still shows free access, contact support with the payment ID, date, amount, and your sign-in email. Please check with us before paying again.

What happens if a payment fails or I close checkout?

An incomplete checkout does not grant premium. If a payment may have completed, check My account and your payment status before retrying. An existing subscription’s renewal authorisation is separate from closing a checkout window; use the cancellation steps above to stop future renewals.

Does cancelling renewal automatically refund an earlier payment?

No. Stopping renewal and refunding a completed payment are separate actions. If you see an unexpected or duplicate charge, or want to ask about a refund, email our support team with the payment details so we can review the issue. The cancellation button does not issue a refund.

Why choose Study With Cat premium?

Premium is for making your study space feel familiar and keeping more of your routine together. Choose the extras you’ll actually use.

Study Kitten · ₹59/month

Choose among three distinct kittens and five fitted accessories. Save up to 20 combinations of scenery, timer settings, kitten, and sound mix so your favourite revision corner is ready to return to. Blend four ambience layers and explicitly back up your club setup and journal to your account.

Scholar Cat · ₹99/month

Includes Study Kitten’s extras, plus subject focus history, daily goals, weekly study reports, and a CSV export. Create or join a private room for up to six Scholar Cat members with a shared focus-and-break timer. Each person joining a shared room needs Scholar Cat membership.

Can I keep studying for free?

Yes. Study Buddy includes the adjustable Pomodoro timer, task list, current wallpapers and music, and a kitten you can name, pet, and play with. A subscription is optional. You can browse the paid kittens and outfits before deciding to subscribe.

Does premium guarantee better grades or more focus?

No plan can promise a particular grade or study result. The paid features add personalisation, saved routines, and tools for reflection or studying together. Your goals and pace stay yours.

Does account backup include all my tasks and music?

Backup is an explicit save-and-restore feature for cat-club settings, saved spaces, and the study journal. It does not upload your free task list or your own music files, and it does not automatically merge devices. Read what the website stores.

How to use a Scholar Cat study room

  1. Everyone signs in with the Google account connected to their active Scholar Cat membership.
  2. The host opens Study with friends, names the room, and chooses Create study room.
  3. The host copies the private invite link and shares it with up to five friends. Each friend opens the link and taps Join room. You can also paste the invite link or code in the room panel.
  4. The host closes the panel and uses the main timer to start, pause, reset, change focus or break mode, or adjust durations. These changes are shared automatically; friends follow the host’s timer.
  5. Your tasks, kitten, wallpaper, and music stay personal. You can refresh or reopen the website: your active room and shared timer reconnect after sign-in.
What if the room disconnects or an invitation does not work?

Open Study with friends and choose Reconnect to room. Temporary connection failures retry automatically. Make sure your invite link or code is complete and every participant has Scholar Cat. A room holds six people including its host and expires 24 hours after creation. If you already belong to another room, reconnect to it and leave before joining the new one.

What happens when the host leaves or closes the tab?

End room closes the room for everyone; a guest’s Leave room only removes that guest. Keep the host tab open for focus and break transitions. If the host closes it, the room remains available until its expiry; the host can reopen the site and reconnect. Guests wait for the host at the end of a timer. Updates normally arrive within five seconds plus connection time, and background tabs refresh when you return.
