    A few things you might be wondering

    How does the cat Pomodoro timer work?

    Choose Focus, add a task, and press Start focusing. The default is 25 minutes of study followed by a 5-minute short break. After four completed focus sessions, the timer prepares a 15-minute long break. Each next session starts when you choose.

    Can I change the study and break times?

    Yes. Click Adjust beside the countdown. Choose whole-minute lengths from 1 to 180 for focus, short breaks, and long breaks. Saving the settings resets the current countdown.

    Can I name my study kitten?

    Click the kitten’s name below the cat, enter a name, and save it. The name is remembered in this browser and used in the companion controls.

    Can I listen to my own study music?

    Yes. Choose Your own music and select an audio file from your device. The file is played locally, loops during the session, and is not uploaded by the website. You will need to choose it again after reloading. Soft keys, rainfall, and brown noise are also included. With Spotify Premium, you can also connect Spotify and play your playlists or liked songs.

    What can I do with the cat during a break?

    Give your kitten a pat or choose Play ball. When a focus timer is running, ball play is paused so the cat can study with you. Another mood lets you browse the different study poses.

    Where are my tasks and settings saved?

    Your tasks, timer settings, kitten name, wallpaper choice, and study totals are stored in this browser. They do not sync between devices, and clearing browser data can remove them. Read the data and privacy notes.

    Premium & payment FAQs

    Know what you’re choosing, how renewal works, and where to find a little help.

    Is Study With Cat free? Why choose premium?

    The Pomodoro timer, tasks, current wallpapers and music, and your interactive study kitten are included in the free Study Buddy desk. Study Kitten adds different cats, fitted outfits, saved spaces, an ambience mixer, and account backup. Scholar Cat adds study reports, goals, and private shared rooms. See which plan fits your routine.

    How much does Study With Cat premium cost?

    Study Kitten costs ₹59/month and Scholar Cat costs ₹99/month, including the first month. Both are recurring monthly subscriptions through Razorpay. The checkout shows the amount and renewal terms before you authorise payment. Read the payment FAQs.

    How do I stop autopay or cancel my subscription?

    Sign in with the Google account used to subscribe, then choose My desk → My account → Stop monthly renewal and confirm. Wait for the confirmation that renewal is stopped. Signing out or closing the website does not cancel autopay. Follow the cancellation steps, including UPI AutoPay help.

    What happens to premium after I stop renewal?

    Your paid features remain available through the paid-access date in My account. After that, the free study desk remains available. Stopping renewal does not automatically refund an earlier payment. Get help with a billing question.

    I paid, but premium hasn’t unlocked. What should I do?

    Check that you are using the same Google account as the purchase, then open My account to refresh your membership. If your bank shows a successful debit and premium still hasn’t appeared, email support with your payment ID, date, amount, and account email before paying again.
